name: Business onboarding AI scope
description: Product expectations for AI assistance across the SMS business onboarding flow.
---

AI assistance should span all three Add a business stages: suggest business type and message purpose from the user's description, draft campaign wording, and draft opt-in and consent wording. Keep the generated content editable. Legal names, addresses, checkbox presence, actual opt-in actions, message frequency, and policy links must remain grounded in user-provided facts; use clear confirmation placeholders for missing details.

**Why:** The user emphasized that assistance should cover the full onboarding flow, including consent.

**How to apply:** When extending onboarding, include useful AI support at each stage while keeping unverifiable business and consent facts for the user to confirm.
